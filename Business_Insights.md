# 🍔 Food E-Commerce Data Analysis – Business Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

This document presents the key business insights and recommendations derived from the Food E-Commerce Data Analysis project using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis focuses on order performance, customer acquisition, customer lifetime value, revenue patterns, delivery efficiency, discounts, and customer satisfaction.

The workbook covers 22,823 transaction records across multiple delivery locations in Bangalore during January–September 2021.

---

## 1️⃣ Order Performance Insights

**Key Findings**

- A total of 22,823 orders were analyzed.
- 22,721 orders were completed successfully.
- 102 orders were cancelled.
- The overall order completion rate was approximately 99.55%.

**Business Interpretation**

The observed completion rate indicates that most recorded orders were successfully completed during the analysis period.

**Recommendations**

- Monitor cancelled orders to understand the reasons behind unsuccessful transactions.
- Track completion rates across delivery areas and time slots.
- Investigate variations in order completion to identify operational issues.

---

## 2️⃣ Customer Acquisition Insights

**Key Findings**

The highest order volumes by acquisition source were:

- Organic: 6,680 orders.
- Google: 5,348 orders.
- Offline Campaign: 2,862 orders.

Organic and Google were the two largest acquisition sources by order volume.

**Business Interpretation**

Organic and Google acquisition channels contributed substantially to the observed order volume.

**Recommendations**

- Monitor acquisition channel performance regularly.
- Compare customer lifetime value across acquisition sources.
- Evaluate marketing effectiveness using both order volume and customer value rather than order count alone.

---

## 3️⃣ Delivery Area Performance

**Key Findings**

- HSR Layout recorded 15,657 orders.
- ITI Layout recorded 3,946 orders.
- Harlur recorded 1,309 orders.

HSR Layout had the highest order volume among the delivery locations.

**Business Interpretation**

Order demand was concentrated in HSR Layout compared with the other listed delivery areas.

**Recommendations**

- Allocate delivery resources according to location-wise demand.
- Monitor order growth across delivery areas.
- Identify locations with increasing order volumes for operational planning.

---

## 4️⃣ Delivery Efficiency Insights

**Key Findings**

- Average delivery time for completed orders was approximately 24.26 minutes.
- Late Night orders recorded an average delivery time of approximately 17.49 minutes.
- Afternoon orders recorded an average delivery time of approximately 25.78 minutes.

**Business Interpretation**

Average delivery duration varied across delivery time slots. Late Night orders had a lower average delivery duration than Afternoon orders in the analyzed data.

**Recommendations**

- Investigate factors contributing to longer delivery times during afternoon periods.
- Monitor delivery performance by month and delivery area.
- Review delivery operations during high-demand time slots.

---

## 5️⃣ Revenue and Order Value Insights

**Key Findings**

- Total Final Amount: 7,963,632.
- Average Final Amount per Order: approximately 348.93.
- Average Product Amount per Order: approximately 352.31.

**Business Interpretation**

The analysis provides an overview of final order amounts and average order value during the observed period.

**Recommendations**

- Monitor average order value across acquisition channels.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and final order amounts.
- Evaluate revenue patterns across different customer segments.

*Currency has not been independently confirmed. The figures are therefore presented without a currency symbol.*

---

## 6️⃣ Customer Satisfaction Insights

**Key Findings**

- Average order rating was approximately 4.85.
- Rating values were available for 17,103 records.

**Business Interpretation**

The recorded ratings provide a way to evaluate customer feedback and explore potential relationships between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

**Recommendations**

- Investigate lower-rated orders.
- Compare ratings across delivery slots and product quantities.
- Examine whether delivery time and delivery charges are associated with rating differences.

---

## 7️⃣ Discount and Delivery Charge Analysis

**Analysis Areas**

The workbook explores:

- Delivery charges as a percentage of product amount by month and delivery slot.
- Discount percentages across delivery slots.
- Discount patterns across delivery areas.
- Relationships between discounts, delivery charges, and customer ratings.

**Business Recommendations**

- Monitor discount percentages across different time slots.
- Compare delivery charges across delivery locations.
- Evaluate whether discount strategies are associated with changes in order completion and average order value.
- Review unusually high delivery charges and discount percentages.

---

## 8️⃣ Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Insights

The workbook includes customer-level LTV calculations and aggregated analysis by:

- Customer acquisition source.
- Customer acquisition month.
- Individual customer.

**Business Recommendations**

- Identify customers with higher observed lifetime value.
- Compare LTV across acquisition channels.
- Use customer value analysis to support customer retention planning.
- Compare acquisition costs with customer value if marketing cost data becomes available.

*Specific highest-LTV customers and acquisition-source rankings are not stated here because those results have not been independently verified.*

---

## 9️⃣ Overall Business Recommendations

**Order Management**
- Monitor completion rates and cancelled orders.
- Identify delivery areas with increasing order demand.

**Marketing Optimization**
- Evaluate acquisition channels using order volume and customer lifetime value.
- Monitor customer acquisition performance over time.

**Delivery Operations**
- Investigate variations in delivery duration across time slots.
- Plan delivery resources based on location-wise demand.

**Revenue Management**
- Monitor average order value and final order amounts.
- Evaluate discount and delivery charge patterns.

**Customer Retention**
- Identify high-value customers through LTV analysis.
- Use customer ratings to identify opportunities for service improvement.

## 🔟 Project Conclusion

The Food E-Commerce Data Analysis project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to analyze e-commerce transactions and delivery operations.

Through 21 business questions, the analysis explores order completion, customer acquisition, customer lifetime value, revenue, discounts, delivery charges, delivery duration, and customer ratings.

The findings provide an analytical foundation for understanding operational performance, customer behavior, and potential business improvement opportunities.
